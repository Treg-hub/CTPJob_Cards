## Entry checklist (each unit)

1. **Start mechanical build** (Aurora tab) — shaft from table; UNN if no sleeve number; set **date** to mech date; fill measurements; toggle **PASS** (red → green); submit.
2. **Electrical queue → Test** — enter cond/ins; **PASS** (Full or Yellow only) or **FAIL** (auto yellow-only spare); set date; submit.
3. **Spares → Install** on the unit number. FAIL / yellow-only spares: **only units 4 & 5** on Aurora (no override if electrical FAIL).

## Notes from paper

- Length is usually **2020 mm** (sometimes written 20.20 / 2020).
- Electrical fail on unit 5 is a good FAIL-path test (quarantine → retest or yellow handling).
- Unit 4/5 are yellow-unit candidates when conductivity is weak or marked yellow only.
