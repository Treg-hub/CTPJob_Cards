# Badenia — Safety & isolation basics (operator pack)

## Roles (OEM)

| Role | Focus |
|------|--------|
| **Operator** | Run production, make-ready, reel change, quality checks at console |
| **Maintenance** | Mechanical/electrical repairs, lubrication to procedure, component replacement |

Do not perform maintenance tasks you are not trained and authorised for.

## Before any hands-on work near moving parts

Typical OEM isolation ideas (always follow the **specific** unit procedure and site LOTO):

1. **Stop** the press with the correct stop control.  
2. Switch the **involved unit / unwinder OFF** on its panel where required.  
3. Close **pneumatic** supply and **discharge residual pressure** where taps are provided.  
4. Close **oil / air** supplies to a printing unit when working on that unit.  
5. **Main switch OFF** when the procedure requires whole-press isolation.  
6. Confirm **no one** is in forbidden areas before restoring power.

Overlooking isolation can cause serious injury or damage.

## Guards and residual risk

- Guards and interlocks exist because residual risks remain.  
- After maintenance that removed a guard: **refit and test** before production.  
- Electrical cabinets: specialised electrical maintenance only.

## Operator stop types (concept)

Know your console lamps/buttons for emergency stop, quick stop, and normal sequences. If status lights show emergency or abnormal stop, **do not force a restart** until the cause is understood.

## Report immediately

- Missing or defeated guards  
- Interlocks taped or jumped  
- Unexpected movement  
- Air or oil leaks at isolation points  

Open a **job card** with machine **Badenia**, clear location, and what you observed.
