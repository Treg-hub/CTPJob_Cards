## What CPS is

The **Cerutti Press Supervisor** on the main console: monitors and keyboards that show press overview, unit settings, presets, and fault/history views.

Keys highlight under the cursor; active switch functions stay **green** when on.

## Where operators go most often

| Need | CPS area (handbook) |
|------|---------------------|
| Overview / main menu | Main diagram of the press |
| Unwinder tension | Unwinder controls |
| **Slitters** position / preset | Ribbons-sorting · slitters position · preset menus |
| Angle bars / ribbons | Ribbons-sorting · position presets |
| **Folder** on/off & production mode | Folder controls · production selection |
| Printing unit temps / humidify / registers | Printing units controls |
| Pulling / tension | Pulling units |
| Job / presets store | Production data · Calculate Job · Store preset |

## Good habits

- Change **one** setting family at a time; confirm the unit you intended (OS/DS, unit number).  
- After a stop: confirm **folder** and **infeed/draw** behaviour before ramping speed.  
- Slitter trips or wrong position: note which slitter and whether trip / position / drive — for the job card.  
- Do not clear faults you do not understand; call electrical/mechanical.

## Job card tips (Aurora)

| Symptom | Location hints |
|---------|----------------|
| No air / leak on turner bars | Folder · turner bar (e.g. TB 2/4/6) |
| Slitter trip / position | Folder · slitters |
| Infeed draw not auto after stop | Units · infeed draw rollers |
| Steam leak on unit | Unit number · steam valve |
