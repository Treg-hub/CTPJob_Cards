# Badenia — Lubrication rules (operator & maint pack)

**In-app only · CTP confidential · Do not photograph, copy, or share outside Job Cards**

Source: Cerutti R135 Job 3570 · lubrication sections and appendix lubricant table.

---

## Hard rules (never break)

1. **Never mix different oils or greases** — same brand or different brands.  
2. **Never mix mineral and synthetic** oils.  
3. If the wrong product was used, **stop and call maintenance** — do not “top up” with something else.  
4. Centralised / auto grease: if grease is **forced out** of points, report **over-greasing** — do not increase volume.

## Why this matters on our floor

Over-greasing and mixed products have shown up on job cards (folder grease purge). Wrong lubricant damages bearings and seals and voids OEM practice.

## If you only do three things

1. Use **only** the product maintenance specifies for that point.  
2. Report **leaks, dry points, and grease blow-out**.  
3. Never invent a substitute “because the drum is empty” — stores/maintenance first.
