# Aurora — Reelstand ARL safety (operator pack)

**In-app only · CTP confidential · Do not photograph, copy, or share outside Job Cards**

Source: vR handling Automatic Reel Loading (ARL) manual · Cerutti Aurora reelstand.

---

## Before you run automatic

- Only **authorised, instructed** operators may run the reel handling system.
- Safety devices must work. If safety is not working properly, **automatic operation is not allowed**.
- **Never disable** safety devices. Working with damaged or defeated safety is forbidden.

## Safety devices (must stay active)

- Emergency stop buttons  
- Bumpers on ARL transfer stage and columns  
- Laser scanner (intrusion)  
- Light barrier (intrusion)

## Shutting down / before maintenance

1. Main switch on power cabinet to **0**.  
2. **Lock with key** when putting out of service or for maintenance/repair.

## Auto vs Maintenance mode

| Mode | Use for |
|------|---------|
| **Auto** | Normal automatic loading when safety is clear and system healthy |
| **Maint** | Maintenance motions, cradle loading/unloading, recovering after certain alarms |

In **Maintenance mode**, **you** are responsible that nobody is in danger.

## Common operator actions

- **Reset laser scanner** — when lamp shows scanner triggered (someone/object in safety area); movements stop until reset.  
- **Reset barrier** — when barrier triggered.  
- **Call operator lamp** — system needs a person at the ARL.  
- **Send reel** — only when preparation is complete and it is safe.

## After measuring / loading alarms

- If reel width measurement disagrees with ordered width: **check the reel**. Reject if wrong; continue only when correct (may require **maintenance mode**).  
- Other alarms during loading: clear the cause, **FAULT/RESET**, then continue loading in **maintenance mode** when the manual procedure requires it.  
- Empty **core basket** when the system alarms; acknowledge only when safe.

## Remember

> Management must ensure safety functions are checked regularly and never put out of order.

If in doubt: **stop**, call maintenance, open a job card — do not bypass interlocks.
