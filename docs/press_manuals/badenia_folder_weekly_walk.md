# Badenia — Folder weekly walk (operator pack)

**In-app only · CTP confidential · Do not photograph, copy, or share outside Job Cards**

Source: Cerutti R135 Job 3570 · Folder PV173 / PV157 ordinary & periodic maintenance guidance.

Use on **both** folders (plant 5/5 and 7/7). Walk with the press **standing still** for access checks, and note anything abnormal **while running** (noise/vibration) without putting yourself at risk.

---

## Weekly checklist

- [ ] **Pneumatics & lube pipes** — integrity, especially rotating/mobile parts on grippers and jaws cylinders  
- [ ] **Grease and oil stations** — running correctly; lubricant reaches all points  
- [ ] **Knife and counterknife** — condition; report wear  
- [ ] **Belts and fingers** (belt box) and acceleration group — integrity  
- [ ] **Safety devices** — pneumatic/electric limit switches; anti-jamming rods intact; no loose parts  
- [ ] **General wear** — anything that will become a breakdown  
- [ ] **Self-cleaning filter** on oil station — manual rotation as required  
- [ ] **Abnormal noise or vibration** during operation — report immediately  

## Every ~500 working hours (or when full) — flag for maintenance

Operators do not always perform these, but **raise a job card** if overdue or failed:

- Folder / delivery **belts**  
- **Tucker blades**  
- Flocked tape on knife / counterknife (or when worn)  
- Empty **condensate** tank on pneumatic accumulator  
- Empty **oil separator** tank (photocell cleaning air)  
- Empty/wash **supply line filter**

## Safety gates & guards (priority)

From plant reliability work: gates, magnetic holds, and guard pistons must work **without bypass**.

- [ ] All safety gates present, close, and interlock  
- [ ] Magnetic switch holds **without tape or bypass**  
- [ ] Guard pistons intact  
- [ ] Nip / sandwich **pressure gauges** readable and mounted  

If a gate or interlock is defeated: **stop**, call management/maintenance, job card — never “run with it taped”.

## When to open a job card

| Finding | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Safety not holding / bypassed | **Immediate** job card + stop if unsafe |
| Gauge unreadable | Job card (cannot set nips correctly) |
| Grease forced out / over-grease | Job card — do not keep pumping |
| Noise, vibration, belt damage | Job card with location (5/5 or 7/7) |

Use **Machine = Badenia**, correct **Location** (folder), clear **description**.
