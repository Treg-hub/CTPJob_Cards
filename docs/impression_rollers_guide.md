# Impression Rollers — Floor Guide

*For Pressroom, Workshop Mechanical, Workshop Electrical, and Admins*

This guide is for the **Imp Rollers** Home tile on the phone. Board review, compliance, and module settings are on **CTP Pulse** (`Impression Rollers`).

Floor staff (No1 / No2 / Foreman, mechanic, electrician) can open the hub **on site only**. Managers and admins may open it off site.

---

## Who does what?

| Role | Typical tasks |
|------|----------------|
| **Pressroom** | On-press map, daily IR / ESA, install after a unit is empty |
| **Mechanical** | Start / complete a build, strip, send-out / receive sleeves |
| **Electrical** | Complete electrical checks (PASS / FAIL) |
| **Workshop managers** | Same as their trade, plus they may attribute work to another clock and adjust event time |
| **Admin** | Full module |

Everyone with access can **see** the on-press map, spares, and outstanding work. **Actions** are scoped to your trade.

---

## Daily idea

1. Open **Imp Rollers** from Home.
2. The **map** shows which roller is on which unit. Yellow units follow the press rules (Badenia/Aurora 4–5; Wifag 1 & 5).
3. **Remove first** if a unit still has a roller. The unit may sit as *awaiting install* until someone installs the next one.
4. Do **not** invent occupancy on the phone — occupancy and roller numbers are saved by the system when you complete a real action.

---

## Mechanical

- **Start build** / **Complete mechanical** from the hub actions.
- Default result is **FAIL** (red). You must tap **PASS** (green) when the check is good.
- Paper **Bearings fitted by** is the mechanical builder.
- **Strip** notifies Workshop Mechanics and Mechanical Managers.
- **Send-out / receive** sleeves when work leaves site.

---

## Electrical

- Complete electrical from the hub.
- Same **FAIL / PASS** toggle (starts on FAIL).
- **PASS** means the unit is suitable for any press. **FAIL** locks the unit to yellow-only units.

---

## Pressroom

- Daily **IR** and **ESA** forms from the hub.
- **Install** only after the unit is empty (remove completed).

---

## Managers (and Admin)

On confirm screens you may:

- Pick **who actually did the work** (department list). Floor staff always record as themselves.
- Adjust the **date/time stamp** on start-build, electrical complete, and install-from-spares.

Do not ask floor staff to pick another person’s clock.

---

## Pulse desk

Use Pulse for life review, compliance, and **Settings → Impression Rollers** (including first-time **Initialize module**, which never wipes occupancy).

---

## What not to do

| Don’t | Do instead |
|-------|------------|
| Install onto a unit that still has a roller | Remove first |
| Leave PASS/FAIL on FAIL if the check passed | Tap **PASS** |
| Invent a roller number in a comment | Use the action screens so the system assigns numbers |
| Open the hub off-site as floor staff | Come on site, or ask a manager |
