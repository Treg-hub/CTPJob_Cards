# Ink Factory — Operator Guide

*For Ink Factory staff on CTP Job Cards*

This guide is **floor capture on the phone**. Ordering, costing, month-end count, and ledger live on **CTP Pulse**.

You must be **on site** to open the Ink hub.

---

## Who does what?

| Role | Typical tasks |
|------|----------------|
| **Ink operator** | Tank dips, Receive Local / IBC, delivery notes, consume IBC, production |
| **Ink manager** | Same capture on the phone, plus **Signed RFO** queue; costing and month-end on Pulse |
| **Lurgi** | Daily Readings (ink + toloul) from the **Lurgi** hub — not Ink stock receive |
| **Admin** | Full access |

Operators **never see money** (WAC, invoice totals). Managers see Pulse costing.

---

## Daily flow

```
  Ink hub
    ├─ Tank % full cards (Y / R / B / K / Binder / Toloul)
    ├─ Expected orders / IBC-pallet ETAs (banner)
    ├─ Receive Local  or  Receive Ink (IBC)
    ├─ After receive: red Pending delivery note → photo + confirm paper DN #
    ├─ Consume IBC into tank (wash litres)
    ├─ Production run (recipe + pots)
    └─ Meter readings (or Lurgi Daily Readings)
```

---

## 1. Tanks

Hub cards show **% full** from dips — not ledger stock value.

- AppBar tank icon → levels, capacity, lows.
- After a **delivery note** on tank materials (toloul / binder), the phone may offer a **post-receive dip**. Colour IBCs top up tanks when you **consume**, not when you receive.

---

## 2. Receive Local (local purchase orders)

1. Open **Receive Local**. Outstanding orders appear after Pulse **Mark sent** (signed RFO + Pastel numbers first).
2. Tap an order. Enter qty received per line. Leave a line blank if it is not on this delivery.
3. Save. Stock is up; you can finish other floor work.
4. Later: **red Pending delivery note** row → photograph the paper DN. On-device OCR may prefill the number (e.g. `WL817898`) — **confirm or correct** before save.
5. Row turns **grey Complete**. You can **Replace DN** until stores pack is sent.

Ad-hoc receive (no order) is an exception and skips the stores pack.

---

## 3. Receive Ink (IBCs / pallets)

1. Pick the **open shipment** you are unloading (ETA chip on the list).
2. Scan or type each IBC. Colour and weight come from the label when possible.
3. Pallet / bulk lines: confirm qty.
4. Same **Pending delivery note** path after the load is saved.

---

## 4. Consume IBC

Transfers a drum into the tank.

- Scan or pick colour. Enter **toloul wash litres**.
- Blank or zero wash: you must confirm no wash was used (flagged for Pulse review).
- Consume also **creates an IBC Bin** in Waste stock for Security to collect.
- Colour tank % increases on consume.

---

## 5. Production

Pick a recipe (CoverWax or Gravure Binder) and pot count. Quantities only — costing stays on Pulse.

---

## 6. Daily Readings

Ink meters + toloul points. Blank = skipped. If some meters are already recorded today, use **Add missing readings** for the rest.

Lurgi staff usually capture this from the **Lurgi** hub.

---

## 7. Managers — Signed RFO

After Pulse creates a PO and RFO PDF, the **Signed RFO** queue on the Ink hub is for photographing the signed paper and entering Pastel RFO # + order #. Pulse cannot mark the order sent until that is done.

---

## Pulse (not this phone)

Ledger, shipments, GRN / stores pack, costing, month-end count, void-and-re-enter.

---

## What not to do

| Don’t | Do instead |
|-------|------------|
| Type a second reading for a meter already done today | **Add missing** only |
| Skip the delivery note after receive | Capture DN from the red row when you can |
| Invent tank % from the ledger | Dip / consume updates tanks |
| Use ad-hoc receive when an order exists | Pick the outstanding order |
