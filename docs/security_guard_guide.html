Security Guard Guide

Site Security — Guard how-to

For Security Guards on the Job Cards phone or gate tablet

This is the boom book. It covers everything you use on the app: signing in, the gate, visitors on foot, who is on site, company cars, blocked vehicles, waste collection, and the locked tablet.

On the phone or tablet: Settings → Documentation → Site Security — Guard Guide.

Printable copy: managers open CTP Pulse → Site Security → Guard how-to and print it for the boom.

Managers use a separate computer desk (CTP Pulse). You do not need that for daily gate work.

At the boom (quick)

  1. Sign in with your clock. Confirm the app shows you.
  2. Open the Security tab. Select your gate first.
  3. Visitor / contractor / transporter in a vehicle → Visitor / Contractor Vehicle.
  4. CTP company car leaving or returning → Company Car (never the visitor card).
  5. Person walking in → On-Foot Visitor.
  6. To see who is still inside → On Site List.
  7. When they leave → Scan out (vehicle) or Sign out (on foot) from that list, or scan the disc again.
  8. Waste truck → switch to the Waste tab.

Scan the 2D barcode (the rectangle of dots), not the thin stripes on the front of a licence.

What Where to scan
Vehicle licence disc The PDF417 on the disc
Driver’s licence The back of the card
Visitor ID (on foot) The back of the ID (optional)

1. What this app is for

You log who comes in and who goes out at the gate:

  • Visitors, contractors, and transporters in vehicles
  • People on foot
  • Company cars (odometer and trip)
  • Who is still on site
  • Waste collections (same phone, Waste tab)

You do not:

  • Create production job cards
  • Change the deny list or Security settings
  • Use the Pulse security desk on a computer

If a tile is missing, you are not signed in as a Security Guard, Site Security is switched off, or you are marked off site. Ask a supervisor.

2. Sign in and home

  1. Open CTP Job Cards.
  2. Sign in with your clock number (and password if asked).
  3. Check the name on screen is yours — never work under someone else’s login.
  4. You should see a simple home: Security and Waste. No Create Job Card / My Work.

On site: Security and Waste only work when the app treats you as on site. If you see a blocked screen, you are off site — go to the factory or ask a supervisor; do not try to log the gate from home.

Notifications: open the inbox from home if a red badge appears (for example a blocked entry your manager should know about). You can still finish the gate job in front of you first.

Updates: if the app says an update is required, install it before you continue. On the locked gate tablet, a supervisor must unlock Kiosk Mode first (see §12).

3. Always choose the gate first

On Security home:

  1. Select Main Gate (or the gate you are standing at). The app remembers it on that device.
  2. Cards stay grey until a gate is chosen.
  3. Change the gate if you move boom.

Every log is stored against that gate. Wrong gate = wrong record.

4. Visitor / contractor / transporter — vehicle IN

Use Visitor / Contractor Vehicle. Do not use this for a CTP company car.

Scan

  1. Tap the card — the disc scanner opens.
  2. Hold the camera on the disc barcode until it beeps and the registration appears.
  3. If the disc is damaged or will not read: tick Disc damaged / cannot scan, then type the plate.
  4. If the typed plate is a company car, the app asks you to Switch — tap Switch and continue on the Company Car screen.

After the disc

  1. Confirm ENTRY.
  2. Choose type: Visitor, Contractor, or Transporter.
  3. Set how many people in the vehicle (default 1). Count everyone, including the driver.
  4. Driver’s licence: the app often opens the licence scanner next. Scan the back of the licence.
    • If you cannot scan: skip only when the app allows it, and pick a reason (No licence / Disc expired / Licence expired / Other).
    • Do not scan the disc again thinking it is a licence — the app will say Incorrect scan.
  5. Check driver name (from the licence, or type it if you skipped).
  6. Host / department — who they are seeing (suggestions appear as you type, e.g. Loading Bay).
  7. Company — their company.
  8. Contractor — if type is Contractor, pick from the list or Add contractor if they are new.
  9. Purpose — e.g. Delivery, Collection. Fill this when the app marks it required.
  10. Photos if you take them.
  11. Submit.

The vehicle should appear under On Site List → Vehicles.

Blocked or expired documents

  • If the plate or name is on the deny list, the app blocks the entry. Do not wave them in. Call a supervisor.
  • Transporter with expired disc or licence: the app blocks.
  • Visitor / contractor with a warning (e.g. expiry): you may continue only with an override reason. That is flagged for the manager.

5. Vehicle OUT

When a visitor / contractor / transporter vehicle leaves:

Option A — from the boom

  1. Visitor / Contractor Vehicle again.
  2. Scan the disc. The app should set EXIT and match who is on site.
  3. If the disc will not read: pick the vehicle from the on-site list.
  4. Confirm occupants leaving (starts at how many came in).
  5. If fewer people leave than came in: the app asks for a note. That is normal (someone staying behind). They must leave later on foot or in another vehicle — log that separately. Submit.

Option B — from On Site List

  1. Open On Site List → Vehicles.
  2. Find the reg. Tap Scan out (you do not need to expand the card first).
  3. Finish the exit on the gate screen.

No driver’s licence is needed on the way out.

6. Company car — leaving and coming back

Use Company Car only. If you started on the visitor card by mistake, Switch when the app offers it.

Leaving (exit)

  1. Scan the disc, or if damaged: tick damaged and pick the car from the list (you cannot type a company-car plate).
  2. Scan the driver’s licence (back) — required on the way out.
  3. Enter clock number from the badge.
  4. Read odometer (km) in the car and type it.
  5. Purpose of trip and destination (address or business name).
  6. Occupants if asked. Submit.

The car is now out on a trip, not “a visitor on site”.

Returning (entry)

  1. Company Car → scan disc (or pick from list).
  2. Enter the return odometer. Mileage is calculated.
  3. Occupants returning: if the count does not match the trip out, add the note the app asks for. Submit.

Costs (managers only)

Guards do not capture fuel/parking/tolls. Supervisors use Add Company Car Cost (receipt photo). If you do not see that tile, skip it.

7. On-foot visitor

Use On-Foot Visitor when there is no vehicle.

  1. Gate already selected.
  2. Scan ID is optional — back of the ID fills the name when it works.
  3. Visitor name is required (check spelling).
  4. Host, company, purpose — same idea as a vehicle visit.
  5. Submit.

They appear under On Site List → Visitors, not on the Vehicles tab.

When they leave: On Site List → Visitors → Sign out → confirm. Or force sign-out only if they left without coming to the boom (see §8).

Two people with the same name can look like one row. Use company/host and expand the card if you are unsure. If it is wrong, ask a supervisor before force signing anyone out.

8. On Site List

This is who is here now, not the full history.

Vehicles tab — grouped: Company Car, Visitor, Contractor, Transporter.
Visitors tab — people who walked in.

Each card shows three lines, for example:

  • Plate · how many people
  • Driver · host or company
  • Since (time) · gate · purpose

Tap the card (not the buttons) to expand full detail: purpose, host, occupants, flags, ID number if scanned. Tap again to close. Only one card stays open.

Button Use
Scan out Vehicle leaving — opens the gate screen with that plate filled
Sign out On-foot visitor leaving
⋮ → Force sign out (no scan) They already left / duplicate / missed scan. You must pick a reason. This is audited.

Search: vehicles by plate or name; visitors by name, host, or company.

Force sign-out reasons: Exit scan not captured · Left without signing out · Duplicate / erroneous entry · Other (type detail).

Do not force sign-out as a shortcut when the person is still at the boom — scan them out properly.

9. Photos

Take photos when the visit needs evidence (load, unusual vehicle, supervisor request). Some sites require photos — the app will not submit until you add them.

Use the in-app camera. Do not skip a required photo to “save time”.

10. No signal / offline

If the network drops, keep logging. The app queues the entry and shows a message such as queued offline. When signal returns, it sends.

Do not write the visit only on paper and skip the app. If submit fails, note the plate/time and retry; tell a supervisor if it stays failed.

11. Waste collection (Waste tab)

Same people often run Waste at this gate. Open the Waste tab — it is not inside Security.

Typical scheduled truck

  1. Loads → Incoming → the scheduled load → Begin Collection.
  2. Driver name and vehicle registration.
  3. Add items: from on-site stock and/or fresh item.
  4. What you type depends on type:
    • Most paper/copper: kg (and count if shown).
    • IBC bins: count only.
    • Used oil / contaminated oil: litres (oil sits in IBCs; you still type litres — no weighbridge for those).
    • Copper skins: two gross bin weights (the app subtracts the empty bin weights).
  5. Photo the loaded truck before it leaves when you can.
  6. Driver signature if the app asks.
  7. Submit Collection.

You do not enter the weighbridge ticket or the rand amount — that is Pulse (manager/admin).

Stock on site: record paper waste sitting in the yard before the truck (Loads → New / Schedule → Paper Waste Stock) so collection is a tick, not a guess.

If the truck was not scheduled, a supervisor may create an on-the-spot load. Guards normally Begin Collection on a Scheduled load.

Full waste pictures and statuses: ask a supervisor for the Waste Recovery — Full Load Guide.

12. Gate tablet (Kiosk Mode)

The main-gate tablet may be locked to Job Cards so nobody can open Chrome or the Android home screen.

  • Use it like the phone: Security + Waste.
  • If you see This device is locked to Job Cards, that is correct.
  • You cannot install an app update while it is locked. Call a supervisor (admin login or the device exit code).
  • After a power cut it should come back into Job Cards by itself. If it sits on the Android home screen, fetch a supervisor — do not “just use Chrome”.

Do not share the kiosk exit code. Do not sign in as someone else “because they know the menus”.

13. What you never do on this login

  • Wave in a deny-list vehicle or person.
  • Log a company car on the visitor card (Switch if the app catches it).
  • Leave people on site overnight without scanning them out or force signing out with a real reason.
  • Change deny list, gates, or settings (computer / admin).
  • Use another person’s clock.

14. If something goes wrong

Problem What to do
Cards grey / “choose a gate” Select the gate at the top of Security home
Scanner blank or wrong Wrong barcode — disc vs back of licence vs ID. Try again. After several fails, type the plate on the damaged-disc path
“Incorrect scan” You scanned a disc when it wanted a licence (or the other way around)
Company car fields on a visitor You scanned a fleet car — tap Switch
Vehicle not on On Site Wrong gate, not submitted, or already scanned out. Search both tabs
On-foot not on Vehicles Look at the Visitors tab
Submit failed Check signal; retry; if queued offline, leave it — it will send
Off-site blocked You must be on site to use Security/Waste
App wants an update Install (unlock kiosk tablet first)

When in doubt: do not invent a second log. Call the Security Manager. A flagged override with a true reason is better than a silent wave-through.

15. End of shift

  • On Site List should match who is actually still inside.
  • Scan out / sign out everyone who left.
  • Force sign-out leftovers with a reason (missed scan, already gone).
  • Sign out of the app if the next person uses the same phone. On the shared tablet, leave Kiosk on; the next guard signs in as themselves.
